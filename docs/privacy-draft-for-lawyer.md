# Privacy Policy `P02`

> `P03` **DRAFT, for a lawyer to review. This is not final and is not yet our official policy.**

## What we collect `P04`

`P05` Your email address and the time you agreed. You give these only in the optional form on the calculator, and we collect them only if you agree (the consent box is ticked). That's all.

## What we don't collect `P06`

`P07` We don't save or send the numbers you enter. The calculation happens on your device, and those numbers are never included in the email form.

`P08` We have no accounts, and no analytics or tracking on this site. The site itself sets no cookies, and our fonts come from our own site, not from other websites.

`P09` If you tap the link to the SEC (sec.gov.ph), you leave this site, and the SEC's website handles your visit.

## Why we collect it `P10`

`P11` To send you the free checklist and the updates about new rules that you asked for, and to keep a record that you agreed.

## Who receives your email address `P12`

`P13` When you send the form, our email service provider, Resend, emails your address and the time you agreed to the site's operator. The operator then adds your address to our mailing list, which is also kept with Resend, and Resend sends our emails. Only your email address and the time you agreed are sent.

## Notes for the lawyer

1. **P08, no tracking and no cookies.** "We have no accounts, and no analytics or tracking on this
   site. The site itself sets no cookies …" describes how the site's own code is built. It must be checked on
   the live site, as hosted, before the page is final.
2. **P18, unsubscribe link.** Confirm that emails sent through Resend include an unsubscribe link.
   The sentence before the blank says every email has one.
3. **P19, deletion.** Each new address is emailed to the site operator's inbox (P13) before it is
   added to the mailing list. A deletion request must also cover that copy in the operator's inbox,
   not only the mailing list.
4. **P14, rate limit.** The email form is protected by a rate limit at the host, Netlify: it counts
   requests per IP address (5 a minute). The visitor's IP address is used for that. This may belong
   in the P14 blank.
