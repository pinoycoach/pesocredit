# Privacy page v1 — short review

Six lines of the peso.credit privacy page are new or reworded and have not had an independent
review yet (BUILD-STANDARD point 14; DECISIONS N45). This document is everything you need to
review them.

## The site and the page

peso.credit is a free calculator. A borrower types in the numbers from their own loan, and the
site shows an estimated interest rate next to the limits published by the Philippine SEC. The
calculation runs on the borrower's own device: nothing they type is saved or sent. For now the site has
no accounts, no forms that send anything, and no email sign-up.

The privacy page is short. From top to bottom: a link back to the calculator, the title
“Privacy Policy”, the lines below, a section “What we don't collect”, a section “Who hosts this
site” and a section “Contact”. The page's other lines were reviewed before and are not part of
this review.

The site is written first for people already struggling with online-lending-app debt: calm, plain,
short sentences; no shame or blame; no false hope; no advice; no accusation.

## One open question: P27

P27 was checked against Netlify's own documents. They support that Netlify processes visitors'
IP addresses as needed to host a site. They do not say that Netlify records every visit, that it
does so to protect the site, or that all web hosts do the same. So this softer wording has been
proposed instead, and is not yet decided:

  > This site is hosted by Netlify. To deliver the site, Netlify processes technical details of each visit, such as your IP address.

Comments on either wording are welcome.
